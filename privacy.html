Klimbora privacy policy

Last updated 10 September 2026

Klimbora does not collect, send or share any information about you.

What the game keeps

Your progress (stars, coins, the skins you own and your best scores) is saved on your own device, in the app's local storage, so it is still there next time you play. It never leaves your device. Uninstalling the game or clearing its data deletes it.

What the game does not do

No accounts, no sign-in, no ads, no analytics, no tracking and no purchases. The game does not ask for your location, contacts, camera, microphone or any other permission, and it works with no internet connection at all.

Children

Because nothing is collected from anyone, nothing is collected from children either.

Questions

Open an issue at github.com/Dan33John64/klimbora.
